## Decision

1. **Rule 1, outcome-claim vocabulary.** VIDS artifacts and tools must not emit certification, attestation, approval, assurance or equivalent outcome claims about a dataset unless an adopted governance decision expressly authorizes that claim. This rule binds dataset-facing outputs: specification text, worked examples, generator output, schema fields, validator output, and public project surfaces.

2. **Rule 2, provenance population.** A generator may populate a provenance field only when the act that field denotes is the act the generator just performed. A generator may transcribe a value from a source record when that source is identified in the resulting record. Transcription does not constitute verification. When neither condition applies, the generator must emit a placeholder or an approved recording-state sentinel.

3. **Both rules bind at the field, not at the tool.** Compliance is evaluated per populated field against the act that field denotes. A tool that performed some act is not thereby licensed to populate fields denoting other acts.

4. **No authorization route is created by this decision.** Rule 1 permits an outcome claim only where a separate adopted governance decision expressly authorizes it. No such decision currently exists, and this decision does not create a general one. In particular, no claim may be justified on the ground that it was "independently established under the governing process": no such process exists.

5. **Governance sign-off records are outside Rule 1.** Approval blocks, signature copies and acceptance records for governance artifacts (MD, CN, SOP, REG, minutes) record who accepted a project decision. They make no claim about a dataset and are not dataset-facing outputs.

## Rationale

The conformance boundary, that VIDS verifies documentation is present and structured and never certifies a dataset, has been carried in practice rather than in the record. Verification on 2026-07-27 confirmed that MD-0001 through MD-0004 establish no vocabulary rule of any kind. Practice is not enforceable and does not survive contributor turnover, which is why certification language reached the specification, the worked examples, the scaffolding generator and the public site simultaneously and stayed there through review. This decision moves the discipline from habit into the record so that a reviewer has a rule to cite.

Rule 2 is bound at the field rather than the generator because a generator-level formulation does not catch the observed defect. The LIDC pipeline had genuinely just performed a format conversion, so any test asking "did this tool perform an act?" returns true and lets a wrongly populated de-identification date through. The question that catches it is narrower: does this specific field denote the act just performed? Field-level binding is the operative difference between a rule that works and one that reads well.

The alternative considered and set aside was to treat each occurrence as an isolated editorial defect and correct the four locations. That was rejected because it leaves the root cause untouched: with no rule in the record, the language returns on the next artifact that nobody thought to check.

Rule 1 is fenced to dataset-facing outputs so the decision does not breach itself. MD-0001 through MD-0004 each carry a sign-off table recording approval, and an unfenced Rule 1 would place the adopting instrument in violation on the day it is accepted.

## Scope

This decision covers what VIDS artifacts and tools may assert. It does not:

- Change any dataset-conformance requirement or validation rule. Only SPEC does that, and only through the ratification path in CONTRIBUTING.md.
- Cover the semantics of `DeIdentification.Tool` or the §8.2 candidate list. That is MD-0007.
- Constrain governance sign-off records, per clause 5.
- Authorize any outcome claim, per clause 4.
- Depend on MD-0007. Rules 1 and 2 are complete without it. MD-0007 applies Rule 1 to one field, so the dependency runs in that direction only. The two ship as one erratum release for release convenience, not because either requires the other.
- Treat `VALIDATION_RULES.md`:60 as a Rule 1 breach. The sentence describes what a third party may find extension fields useful for and then disclaims VIDS validation of them. It asserts nothing about a dataset, so Rule 1 does not reach it. It is corrected in the same release because "audit" is not project vocabulary, and the Conformance Boundary section is the wrong place to seed it. Recorded here so the judgment is deliberate rather than inferred from the correction.
- Address the emptiness WARN work. Q002 is held behind this decision because it targets the §11.1 structure being changed here; S002, Q003 and M002 touch nothing in this scope and proceed.
