## Decision

Effective the acceptance date recorded above:

1. **Recording, not constituting.** This decision records the current composition of the VIDS Steering Committee. It does not create a new committee.

2. **Composition.** The Steering Committee consists of: Dr. Joan S. Muthu, Co-Founder and CTO; John Shalen R., Co-Founder and COO; John Xavier, Co-Founder and Head of US and Global Operations.

3. **Signatory authority.** All Steering Committee members hold governance signatory authority.

4. **Membership recording rule.** Future appointments, resignations, removals, or changes to Steering Committee membership shall be recorded through a Maintainer Decision. Each such decision applies only from its stated effective date.

5. **Prior decisions unaffected.** MD-0001 through MD-0004 remain exactly as approved. They are not amended, re-signed, or reinterpreted.

6. **Charter update.** GOVERNANCE.md shall be updated to reflect the current Steering Committee composition, state the rule in clause 4, and reference this decision.

## Rationale

VIDS governance is transitioning from its initial operating structure to a permanent governance model. This decision formally records the Steering Committee membership, establishes signatory authority for future governance decisions, and defines how future membership changes will be documented.

## Application

GOVERNANCE.md gains a Steering Committee Membership provision reading: "The current composition of the Steering Committee is recorded through Maintainer Decisions (MDs). Appointments, resignations, and removals are recorded by subsequent MDs, each applying from its stated effective date." The principal list is updated to match clause 2, with a charter change-log entry referencing MD-0005.

Subsequent MDs, appointment confirmations, and governance instruments carry the signatory set in clause 3. The public website governance page is updated on the effective date.

## Scope note

This decision applies only to VIDS governance. It does not change: previous governance decisions; publication authorship, which reflects contribution as declared at submission; commercial signing authority for Princeton Medical Systems contracts; Princeton Medical Systems partnership agreements; or the operation and attribution of organizational accounts, which are infrastructure and are not attributed to individuals.
