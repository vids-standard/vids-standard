## Decision

The Steering Committee adopts the simplified GOVERNANCE.md and CONTRIBUTING.md merged in this pull request as the current governance policy and contribution process for VIDS, replacing the prior versions of both documents.

With this adoption:

1. The Governance Decision Lifecycle process, including Signature Copies, sign-off tables, and separate approval-evidence files, is retired. Maintainer Decisions are accepted as described in GOVERNANCE.md Section 9: required Steering Committee approval recorded on the pull request, then merge. GitHub is the approval record.
2. The document taxonomy operating model adopted under MD-0002 and its administrative consequences are superseded, including the requirement to maintain REG-DOC and the planned migration of `requirements.yaml` into a REG-REQ governance artifact. Existing technical traceability files remain available under GOVERNANCE.md Section 12; no registry designation is required for them.
3. REG-DOC is retired as a maintained register, consistent with GOVERNANCE.md Section 16. Its final version is marked Superseded and retained in the repository for history. The repository itself is the record of governing artifacts.
4. MD-0001 through MD-0007 and CN-0001 remain accepted historical records. Their substantive decisions remain in effect except where restated or superseded by the new GOVERNANCE.md: the Specification-over-validator principle of MD-0001 is restated in Sections 2 and 3; the reference-implementation publication policy of MD-0003, the Governance Handbook adopted under MD-0004, and the assertion discipline of MD-0006 remain in effect. MD-0005 remains the historical record of the current Steering Committee composition, restated in Section 4; its prior signatory mechanics and its rule that membership changes must be recorded through a Maintainer Decision are superseded by GOVERNANCE.md Sections 7, 9, and 16.
5. The Governance Handbook may continue to state member guidance and substantive member expectations, but it does not add mandatory governance procedures beyond GOVERNANCE.md. Where the Handbook prescribes additional approval, voting, signature, filing, appointment, or decision-record mechanics, GOVERNANCE.md governs. A Handbook refresh is a separate later action.
6. Two draft decisions from the 2026-08-17 launch package, never Proposed and never filed, are dispositioned as follows. Because they were never Proposed or filed, they did not reserve MD numbers. The seat-floor bootstrap draft is retired as moot: it existed to resolve a conflict between Handbook Section 8 and the three-member Steering Committee, and GOVERNANCE.md Section 7 now gives the three-member Committee full decision authority directly, with clause 5 above resolving the Handbook conflict. The Advisory Council appointment record draft is returned to the Steering Committee for handling under GOVERNANCE.md Section 14. If written acceptance and an appropriate appointment record are already sufficient under Section 14, no MD is required. If the Committee chooses to create an MD, it uses the next unused number.

## Authority for this amendment

This MD records the governance amendment; it does not supply the authority to amend GOVERNANCE.md. The amendment is approved under the Steering Committee consensus rule in force before merge: all three current Steering Committee members approve this pull request. On merge, the simplified GOVERNANCE.md becomes effective and governs subsequent decisions, which require approval from two eligible Steering Committee members per its Section 7.

## Reason

VIDS is stewarded by a three-person team. The prior process carried signature ceremonies, duplicate registers, and separate evidence files whose weight was not matched to a demonstrated need. GitHub already records proposals, reviews, approvals, and merges. The simplified model preserves clear authority, review of substantive changes, public comment on breaking changes, and full traceability, while removing administrative process that duplicated the repository record.

## Effective

On merge of this pull request.
