## Decision

During the PR #3 governance investigation, the maintainers concluded that the project needs an explicit document taxonomy. The taxonomy - the Normative Principle, the charter/artifact model, the five artifact types (SPEC, MD, CN, SOP, REG), and their lifecycles, visibility, authority, and enforcement - is defined in **`GOVERNANCE.md`**. This decision adopts that model as the project's governance framework.

Two prior open questions are resolved by this adoption: the **REG** (registry) type is adopted now rather than deferred, and **MD** is scoped to *Maintainer Decision* (public standard governance), with internal company decisions out of scope. GOVERNANCE.md is the definition of record; this decision does not restate it.

## Consequences

- Retro-stamp **MD-0001** and **CN-0001** with the standard header block (type, status, normative flag, visibility).
- Create **`REG-DOC`** - the index of all Layer 1 artifacts.
- Migrate the §3.2 traceability material (`requirements.yaml`, requirements inventory) under **`REG-REQ`**.
- Add a "Document Types" cross-reference from **CONTRIBUTING.md** to GOVERNANCE.md.

## Remaining open question

Whether an MD needs a distinct pre-decision (RFC) form before acceptance, or whether the existing GitHub issue + `spec-change` label serves that role. Left for a maintainer decision; not blocking this adoption.

## Self-reference note

This decision adopts the taxonomy; it does not define it. `GOVERNANCE.md` (a charter document) defines it. An MD is authorized by the charter, not by itself - so there is no circular dependency.
